Who We Are

Odyssey Permits is an independent permitting service dedicated to helping trucking companies, owner-operators, freight brokers, and heavy haul carriers obtain Oversize (OS) and Overweight (OW) permits throughout the continental United States.

Our goal is simple: provide fast, accurate, and dependable permitting solutions so our customers can stay compliant with state regulations and keep their loads moving safely and efficiently.

What We Do

OS/OW Permits

Processing permits for oversize and overweight commercial loads.

Multi-State Permits
