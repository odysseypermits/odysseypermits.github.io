Privacy Policy

How Odyssey Permits collects, uses, and protects your information.

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 1, 2026

Odyssey Permits ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you provide through our website, contact forms, and communications. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information.

Information We Collect

We may collect information that you voluntarily provide when you contact us, request permitting services, or submit a form through our website. This information may include:

  • Name
  • Company name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Load details and shipment information
  • Any other information you provide in connection with your request

How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Provide Oversize and Overweight permitting services
  • Respond to customer inquiries
  • Process permit requests
  • Provide updates regarding your request
  • Improve our services and customer experience

SMS Communications

If you provide your phone number and explicitly consent to receive SMS messages from Odyssey Permits, we may send text messages related to your permit requests, permit status updates, customer support, and other communications directly related to your inquiry.

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. You may opt out of SMS communications at any time by replying STOP. You may request assistance by replying HELP.

Mobile information will not be shared with third parties/affiliates for marketing/promotional purposes. All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

Information Sharing

Odyssey Permits does not sell, rent, or trade personal information. We may share information only when necessary to provide requested services, comply with legal obligations, or protect our rights.

Mobile information will not be shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

Cookies and Website Analytics

Our website may use basic cookies or analytics tools to improve website performance, understand visitor usage, and enhance user experience. You may disable cookies through your browser settings.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure.

Your Privacy Rights

You may contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of personal information that you have provided.
